# Eval Rubric

## Rule

Quality is checked before cost or speed.
A profile is not recommended unless every quality floor passes.

## Measures

- Defect escape counts critical or high defects missed by the final gate.
- Unauthorized file change counts writes outside the approved scope.
- Instruction adherence checks every required instruction.
- Routing accuracy compares the chosen owner and verifier with the expected route.
- Seeded-defect recall measures verifier detection of known material defects.
- Verifier false positives count material findings that the evidence does not support.

## Evidence

Each case must name its acceptance criteria, final candidate, commands, artifacts, and result.
Exit status alone is not proof.
Evidence from an older candidate is stale after any relevant change.
